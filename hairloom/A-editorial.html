About

A salon that still likes haircuts.

Hairloom is a high-end salon in Takapuna. We cut women’s hair as the main work of the floor, and we cut men’s hair with the same care. Colour sits beside the cut — including tie dye haircuts when that is the brief.

There is no existing site to hide behind. The proof is in the chair: experience, and the fact we like both the haircut and the person sitting for it.

Premium, local, and friendly — on purpose.
