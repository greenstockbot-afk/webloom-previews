What we build

Soft and hard landscape, installed.

Home owners come to us when the backyard needs more than a tidy-up — when the ground, structure and planting have to work together.

01

Decking

Hardwood and composite decks set true to the house — posts, bearers, boards and balustrades finished so you can use them the week we leave.

02

Retaining & block walls

Timber and block retaining that holds the cut. Drainage, backfill and levels first — planting only after the wall is doing its job.

03

Concrete

Paths, pads, steps and outdoor floors. Formwork, pour and finish with clean edges — a surface built to last Auckland weather.

04

Fencing

Boundary and screen fences posted to the line. Straight rails, even palings, gates that swing true.

05

Excavating & soiling

Cut and fill for the build. Levels set before anything else goes in, then soil and mulch where planting belongs.

06

Planting & mulching

Trees, hedges and garden beds after the hard work is in. Planting that sits on a constructed site — not a sketch over soft ground.
