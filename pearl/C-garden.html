About

Pearl Landscapes finishes residential gardens across Auckland. We set the hard landscape first — timber, concrete, fence, and wall — then plant, soil, and mulch so the garden can settle. The work is local. We stay with a site until it looks as if it has always been there.

What we do

Every trade we bring to an Auckland garden, from the first excavation to the last barrow of mulch.

  • DeckingTimber platforms and edges that sit level with the planting.
