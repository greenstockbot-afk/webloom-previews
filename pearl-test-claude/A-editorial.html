What We Build

Hard and soft landscaping, handled by one crew.

From structural retaining and poured concrete to planting and topsoil — we cover the full scope on every project we take on. No sub-contracting. No coordination headaches.

01

Decking

Hardwood, kwila, and composite decking installed to last. Structural framing, post setting, balustrade, and LBP consent work all included.

02

Concrete

Poured paths, driveways, patios, and entertainment areas with broom, exposed aggregate, or polished finishes.

03

Fencing

Timber paling, feature timber, steel, and aluminium fencing built to boundary-compliant standards. Posts set in concrete.

04

Retaining Walls

Concrete block, mortared stone, and timber retaining structures engineered and built to reshape any Auckland section.

05

Planting & Soiling

Native and exotic planting programmes with quality topsoil, compost preparation, mulching, and ongoing maintenance options.

06

Excavation

Full residential earthworks, digger hire, spoil removal, and site clearance. We have the gear and the team to move anything.

About Pearl Landscapes

"We started Pearl Landscapes to answer one problem: Auckland homeowners juggling five different trades to build one backyard."

We handle the full scope — from earthworks to the last planted native. That means less coordination for you, and a finished result that actually holds together as a whole.

We work across the city's best residential postcodes on projects that matter to the people who live there. Our crew are full-time employees, not subcontractors, and we care about the finish as much as the client does.
