About Pearl Landscapes

Built to handle the whole job.

Pearl Landscapes was started to solve a real problem: Auckland homeowners spending months coordinating five separate trades just to get one backyard done. We decided there was a better way.

Our crew handles every element of a residential build in-house — earthworks, concrete, decking, fencing, retaining, planting. When you work with Pearl, you're dealing with one team from first dig to the last planted native.

Auckland-based and family owned since 2012. Our crew are full-time employees who have built some of Auckland's best residential backyards. We're proud of the work, and we stand behind it.
