What We Build

Hard and soft landscaping, handled in full.

From structural earthworks through to the last planted native — Pearl takes on the complete scope so your project moves as one, not as five separate jobs.

i

Decking

Hardwood, kwila, and composite decking installed to last. Structural framing, balustrade, and LBP consent work included.

ii

Concrete

Poured paths, driveways, patios, and entertainment areas. Broom, exposed aggregate, or polished finishes.

iii

Fencing

Timber paling, feature timber, steel, and aluminium fencing. Posts set in concrete, built to boundary-compliant standards.

iv

Retaining Walls

Concrete block, mortared stone, and timber retaining built to reshape any Auckland section. Structural and decorative.

v

Planting & Soiling

Native and exotic planting programmes with quality topsoil, compost, mulching, and ongoing care options.

vi

Excavation

Full residential earthworks, digger hire, and spoil removal at any scale. We have the equipment and the crew.

Full Backyard Build

Built for homeowners who want the whole thing done.

Pearl Landscapes was founded on a straightforward observation: the trades involved in building a backyard — earthworks, concrete, decking, fencing, planting — rarely talk to each other well. Homeowners carry the load of coordinating five different crews, timelines, and invoices.

We fixed that. Pearl handles the full scope in-house. Our crew are full-time employees who work together every day, and our projects move faster, cleaner, and with less stress for the people who live there.

Auckland-based since 2012. Family owned. Fully licensed.
