How the build unfolds

A straightforward way through a complex job.

01

Walk the site

We meet at your home, hear how you want to use the space and check access, levels, existing structures and drainage.

02

Define the build

You receive a practical scope covering materials, sequence and cost. If the job needs engineering or consent, we flag it early.

03

Build in order

Earthworks first, structures and surfaces next, then soil, planting and mulch. One crew keeps every junction accountable.

04

Hand back the garden

We clean down, walk through the completed work and leave clear care guidance for the new planting and materials.
