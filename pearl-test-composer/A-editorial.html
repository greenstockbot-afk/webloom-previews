What we build

Soft and hard landscape — installed, not imagined.

01

Decking

Hardwood and composite decks with correct fall, fixings, and handrails. Built to walk on the week we finish.

02

Retaining & block walls

Timber, block, and concrete retaining that holds the cut. Drainage behind the wall before anything goes in front.

03

Concrete & paving

Paths, pads, steps, and exposed aggregate. Formwork set true, edges clean, surfaces that survive Auckland rain.

04

Fencing

Boundary, pool, and screen fencing. Posts set deep, rails level, palings even — built to the survey line.

05

Excavation

Cut, fill, and level before the build starts. Machine dig with hand finish where the house meets the ground.

06

Planting & soil

Soil conditioning, mulching, hedges, and trees after the hard work is in. Planting that sits on a constructed site.

Our approach

Capable hands. Straight talk.

We build backyards the way a good tradie should — structure first, finish last.

Home owners come to us when the job is bigger than mowing and tidying. They want a deck that ties into the house, a wall that stops the bank slipping, concrete that drains, and planting that survives the first winter.

Pearl Landscapes runs as a construction crew, not a design studio. We measure the site, price the work clearly, and turn up with the right gear. Auckland clay, tight access, sloping sections — we have done it before.
