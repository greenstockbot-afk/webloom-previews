Services

Everything the backyard needs.

Decking, concrete, retaining, fencing, excavation, planting — priced separately or as one integrated build. No hand-offs between trades.

01

Decking

Timber and composite decks engineered for Auckland weather. Posts, bearers, boards, balustrades — finished and ready to use.

02

Retaining walls

Block, timber, and concrete retaining that holds the bank. Ag-line, backfill, compaction — structure before aesthetics.

03

Concrete & paving

Paths, driveways, steps, and exposed aggregate. Formed, poured, and finished with clean edges that drain properly.

04

Fencing

Boundary, pool, and privacy fencing. Posts set in concrete, rails true, gates that swing square.

05

Excavation

Cut and fill, trenching, and site prep. Machine work with hand finish around foundations and existing structures.

06

Planting & soil

Topsoil, mulch, hedges, and specimen planting after the hardscape is locked in. Gardens that survive Auckland clay.

Our story

A crew that stays until it is finished.

Pearl Landscapes started because home owners were tired of coordinating five different trades for one backyard. Digging here, decking there, a fencer who never showed — we built a team that does the lot.

We are Auckland locals. We know the clay, the council rules, and the access headaches on a steep Ponsonby section. We quote honestly, turn up on time, and leave a site you can actually live in.
