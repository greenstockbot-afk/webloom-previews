After the last cut

The good lawn is the one you notice only when you look for it. Even height. Clean edge. Hedge held. We work the round so that by evening the mower is already on the trailer and the section is sitting still. Auckland grows fast. The work is to be ahead of that, not chasing it on Saturday.
