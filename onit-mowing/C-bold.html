How a quote works

Send the suburb, a rough size, and whether you want the weekly, the hedge, or the lot. We look at the section, we send a number, we pick a day that can live on the round.

If the grass is a month gone, the first visit is a reset. After that it is a weekly, and the lawn stops being the thing you argue about on Saturday morning.
