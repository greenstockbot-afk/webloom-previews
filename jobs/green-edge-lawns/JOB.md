## Photo Placeholder Notes

**No real Green Edge job photos were available at build time.** All gallery and hero imagery uses high-quality Unsplash stock photography of lawns, mowing, edging, hedges, and property grounds. Each concept labels placeholders honestly:

- **look-a:** Gallery section notes "Representative imagery below — real job photos to be added when available." Hero caption reads "Stock imagery — representative of our lawn care standard."
- **look-b:** Film-strip gallery footer note: "All gallery images are high-quality stock photography — placeholders until Green Edge job photos are available."
- **look-c:** Gallery section note: "Stock photography placeholders — real job photos coming soon."

### Recommended replacements when client photos arrive
1. Hero — freshly mown residential lawn (stripes visible)
2. Commercial — berm or large-property ride-on mow
3. Edging — close-up of crisp path/driveway edge
4. Hedge pruning — before/after hedge line
5. Clean up — overgrown section restored
6. Crew shot — Byron or team on site with equipment (builds trust)
