Our story

Built for people who notice the details.

gg began as a small table of tools and planters — things we actually used in our own garden. Neighbours asked where they came from. We kept sourcing better.

Today the shop carries three dozen pieces we would put in our own homes: nothing trendy for a season, nothing that falls apart by autumn.

Every order ships with care notes. Every enquiry gets a real reply — not a bot, not a template.
