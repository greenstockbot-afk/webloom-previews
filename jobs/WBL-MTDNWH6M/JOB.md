# WBL-MTDNWH6M — gg retail concepts

## Brief summary

Three presentation-ready one-page website concepts for **gg**, a bold, energetic, friendly local retail / ecommerce brand selling curated home & garden goods (11–50 SKUs). Primary conversion: **get quote / send enquiry**. Features: online checkout, catalogue, gallery/portfolio, online booking. Brand personality: bold, energetic, friendly, local. Buyer-supplied founder portrait used as brand imagery.

| Folder | Concept |
|--------|---------|
| `look-a/` | **Editorial Luxury** — ivory paper, Instrument Serif + Manrope, asymmetric editorial grid, restrained sage/navy palette |
| `look-b/` | **Cinematic Premium** — midnight void, Cormorant Infant + Outfit, full-bleed hero, layered dark atmosphere, gold accents |
| `look-c/` | **Contemporary Bold** — coral/lime/blue, Syne + Space Grotesk, broken mosaic grid, marquee ticker, energetic asymmetry |
